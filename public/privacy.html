NexBot 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-24 · 运营方：NexorAI · 联系：darrenhoomessi@gmail.com

NexBot（bot.nexorai.com.cn）是一个个人 AI 助理服务。本政策说明我们收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及你对它们的控制权，并特别说明连接 Gmail 时的处理方式。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 账号信息：你用 NexorAI 账号（user.nexorai.com.cn）登录时的账号标识和邮箱。
  • 你的内容：对话、附件、记忆、例行任务，以及你配置的模型凭据和连接器凭据。凭据加密保存在你的工作区。
  • 运行记录：用于排错和安全的服务日志与审计记录。

2. 连接 Gmail 时

只有你主动点击“连接 Gmail”并在 Google 页面同意后才会发生。

我们请求的权限

  • 仅 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly（只读）。
  • 我们不能发送邮件、创建草稿、修改或删除邮件、修改标签。当前版本也不下载附件内容，不做后台同步。

我们访问和保存什么

  • 邮箱地址：用于区分你连接的邮箱，并显示在设置里。
  • 授权凭据（访问令牌、刷新令牌）：加密保存在你的工作区保险库，用于代你调用 Gmail。
  • 邮件内容：不会被批量同步或预先保存。只有当你已明确授权的 Agent 在执行你的任务时调用 gmail.search、gmail.read、gmail.thread，才会读取相关邮件的主题、发件人、收件人、日期、摘要、标签，以及正文（单封最多约 24,000 字符）和附件的名称、类型、大小。

这些内容会去哪里

  • 读取到的内容会交给该 Agent，并发送给你为该 Agent 配置的 AI 模型服务商，用来完成你的请求。不同服务商有各自的隐私条款。
  • 读取结果会作为这次任务的工具调用记录，保存在你的工作区数据库和对话历史里，直到你删除相关对话或要求我们删除。服务器的运维备份也会包含这些数据。
  • 每个账号有独立的工作区，其他用户看不到你的邮箱连接、凭据或邮件内容。每个 Agent 须由你逐个授权，连接 Gmail 本身不会给任何 Agent 访问权限。

我们的承诺（Google API 受限使用）

NexBot 对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用和向其他应用的转移，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括受限使用要求。具体而言：

  • 只在你请求的功能中使用 Gmail 数据，不用于其他目的。
  • 不出售 Gmail 数据，不用于广告、用户画像或个性化广告投放。
  • 不使用 Gmail 数据训练通用的或非个性化的 AI 模型。
  • 不允许人工阅读你的 Gmail 数据，除非：你明确同意、为排查安全问题或滥用、为遵守法律，或数据已被聚合并匿名化用于内部运营。
  • 不与第三方共享，除非是上述“AI 模型服务商”（按你的配置，为完成你的请求）、为提供服务所需的基础设施服务商，或法律要求。

3. 服务商与存储位置

服务运行在日本东京的云服务器上，经 Cloudflare 提供 HTTPS 访问。登录由 NexorAI 账号服务提供。你选择的 AI 模型服务商会接收你发给模型的内容。

4. 保留与删除

  • 断开 Gmail：在 设置 → 连接器 中断开，本地凭据和对 Agent 的授权会立即删除，并尝试向 Google 撤销授权。已写入对话历史的内容不会因断开而自动删除。
  • 在 Google 撤销：你也可以随时在 Google 账号的第三方访问页面撤销 NexBot 的访问。
  • 删除数据：删除相关对话，或发邮件给我们请求删除你的账号数据。我们会先核实你对该账号的控制权。为安全、防滥用或法律要求，可能保留最少量记录。

5. 安全

凭据在保存时加密，传输使用 HTTPS。没有任何系统可保证绝对安全。

6. 儿童

本服务不面向 16 岁以下的儿童。

7. 变更与联系

政策有重大变化时，我们会更新本页日期。问题、数据副本或删除请求，请联系 darrenhoomessi@gmail.com。请不要在邮件里发送密码、令牌或验证码。

NexBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24 · Operator: NexorAI · Contact: darrenhoomessi@gmail.com

NexBot (bot.nexorai.com.cn) is a personal AI assistant service. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the controls you have, with specific detail on the Gmail connector.

1. Information we handle

  • Account data: the identifier and email of the NexorAI account (user.nexorai.com.cn) you sign in with.
  • Your content: conversations, attachments, memories, routines, and the model and connector credentials you configure. Credentials are stored encrypted in your workspace.
  • Operational records: service logs and audit records used for troubleshooting and security.

2. When you connect Gmail

This happens only if you choose “Connect Gmail” and approve access on Google’s consent screen.

Permission requested

  • Only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (read-only).
  • NexBot cannot send email, create drafts, modify or delete messages, or change labels. This version also does not download attachment contents or sync in the background.

What we access and store

  • Your email address, to tell mailboxes apart and show the connected account in settings.
  • Authorization credentials (access and refresh tokens), stored encrypted in your workspace vault, used to call Gmail on your behalf.
  • Email content is not bulk-synced or pre-stored. Only when an Agent that you have explicitly authorized runs gmail.search, gmail.read or gmail.thread for your task does NexBot read the relevant messages: subject, sender, recipients, date, snippet, labels, the body (up to about 24,000 characters per message), and attachment names, types and sizes.

Where that content goes

  • It is given to that Agent and sent to the AI model provider you configured for that Agent so it can complete your request. Each provider has its own privacy terms.
  • The result is saved as the task’s tool-call record in your workspace database and conversation history until you delete the conversation or ask us to delete it. Operational server backups also contain this data.
  • Each account has its own workspace; other users cannot see your mailbox connection, credentials or email content. You authorize each Agent individually; connecting Gmail alone gives no Agent access.

Google API Limited Use disclosure

NexBot’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use Gmail data only to provide the features you request, and for no other purpose.
  • We do not sell Gmail data, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or serving personalized ads.
  • We do not use Gmail data to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI models.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Gmail data unless you give consent, it is necessary for security or abuse investigation, it is required by law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.
  • We do not share it with third parties except the AI model provider you configured (to complete your request), infrastructure providers needed to run the service, or as required by law.

3. Providers and location

The service runs on cloud servers in Tokyo, Japan, and is served over HTTPS through Cloudflare. Sign-in is provided by the NexorAI account service. The AI model provider you choose receives the content you send to the model.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnect Gmail in Settings → Connectors: local credentials and Agent grants are removed immediately and we attempt to revoke the grant with Google. Content already written into conversation history is not automatically deleted by disconnecting.
  • Revoke at Google at any time on your Google Account third-party access page.
  • Delete data by deleting the relevant conversations, or by emailing us to delete your account data. We will first verify that you control the account. We may keep the minimum records needed for security, abuse prevention or legal obligations.

5. Security

Credentials are encrypted at rest and traffic uses HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Children

The service is not directed to children under 16.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above. For questions, a copy of your data or deletion requests, contact darrenhoomessi@gmail.com. Please never send passwords, tokens or verification codes by email.
